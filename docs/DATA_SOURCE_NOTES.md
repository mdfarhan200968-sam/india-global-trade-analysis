# Data source notes

- Official annual source: Government of India Economic Survey 2025-26, Statistical Appendix, Table 6.1(B).
- Source agency: Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence & Statistics (DGCI&S), Kolkata.
- Selected period: FY2020-21 to FY2024-25.
- FY2024-25 is provisional in the source table.
- For country/commodity detail, use the official TradeStat portal and preserve downloaded source files and extraction dates.
